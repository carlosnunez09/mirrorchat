Privacy

Last updated November 2026

MirrorChat has no accounts. You get a random name, and the site keeps as little as it can. This page says exactly what is stored and who can see it.

What stays in your browser

  • Your chat name, the room you last used, and whether sound is on. These live in your browser's local storage and never leave your device unless you post.
  • There are no cookies, no ads and no analytics.

Public rooms

  • Messages you post in a room are saved in our database with your name, the room and the time.
  • Everyone in that room can read them. They are not private, and they stay until they are removed.
  • Do not post anything you want to keep private.

Random 1-to-1 text and video

  • Messages in a random 1-to-1 text chat, and the text box beside a video call, go only to your partner. We do not store them.
  • Video and sound travel directly between your browser and your partner's, and are encrypted. We do not record or store them.
  • To pair you, a temporary random ID is kept in a waiting list for up to about 25 seconds.
  • When you press Report, we store your temporary ID, the other person's temporary ID and the time, so abuse can be reviewed.

Your IP address

In a direct video call, your partner's browser can see your IP address. This is how internet video works. If the call has to go through a relay server instead, the relay sees it and your partner does not. If this worries you, use a VPN.

Services MirrorChat uses

  • Supabase stores room messages and reports and carries live messages.
  • Cloudflare hosts the site and, when a direct video connection fails, relays the call.
  • Google provides the STUN servers that help two browsers find each other, and the fonts. Google sees your IP address when these load.
  • jsDelivr serves the Supabase code library.

Removing a message

Because there are no accounts, we cannot tell whose message is whose beyond the name shown. If a message in a public room should be removed, tell the site owner what it says and which room it is in.

Children

MirrorChat is not for children. Video chat and random 1-to-1 text ask you to confirm you are 18 or older.
