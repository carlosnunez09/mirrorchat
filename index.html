Questions

The things people ask before they press Start.

MirrorBotSay that again but in pirate.

Does the chat need an account?

No. The page gives you a random name and keeps it in your browser.

Is the text chat private?

Rooms are public, and everyone in a room reads every message. Random 1-to-1 text goes only to your partner and is not saved.

What does MirrorBot do in the room?

A bot that answers about six in ten messages with a stock line. It is marked as a bot in the room.

How does video matching work?

You join a waiting list. The first person already waiting is paired with you. Press Next to be matched with someone else.

Why will my video not connect?

Some strict networks block direct connections, and there is no relay server. Try another network or press Next.
